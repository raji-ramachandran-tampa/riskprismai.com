  1. 1970LawBank Secrecy Act

    Financial crime reporting and recordkeeping.

  2. 2002LawSarbanes–Oxley (SOX)

    Financial reporting controls for covered issuers.

  3. 2004Global / Basel frameworkBasel II Pillar 2 (ICAAP)

    Internal capital adequacy assessment and supervisory review.

  4. 2010LawDodd–Frank Act

    Statutory basis for Dodd–Frank Act Stress Testing (DFAST).

  5. 2011Supervisory exerciseFirst CCAR exercise

    Capital planning and stress scenarios for large bank holding companies.

  6. 2014International accounting standardIFRS 9

    Expected credit loss impairment for institutions reporting under IFRS.

  7. 2016U.S. accounting standardCECL (ASU 2016-13)

    Current expected credit losses for in-scope U.S. GAAP financial assets.

  8. 2019Singapore / Updated principlesMAS FEAT

    Fairness, ethics, accountability and transparency in financial-sector AI and data analytics.

  9. 2019Hong Kong / Supervisory principlesHKMA AI principles

    Governance and controls for banks using AI.

  10. 2024EU regulationEU AI Act

    Entered into force in 2024. Requirements apply in phases.

  11. 2025LawGENIUS Act

    Payment stablecoin issuer framework.

  12. 2026Supervisory guidanceSR 26-2

    Risk-based model oversight. Superseded SR 11-7.

  13. 2026U.S. / OCC examiner resourceOCC Super Intelligence framework

    November 2026 framework for evaluating bank AI use. It introduces no new enforceable standards.
