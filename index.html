What we do

Six places AI pays off.
Each one governed.

We combine domain expertise with large language model (LLM) tools and conventional statistical methods, using whichever fits the problem better. LLMs draft, extract, summarize and recommend. Credit, compliance and customer decisions stay with accountable people at your institution.

01 / CREDIT DECISIONING

Faster, more consistent credit decisions

Scorecards, decision rules and credit policy for consumer, small-business and commercial lending, with challenger testing.

Where AI helps
  • Extracting data from financial statements, tax returns and bank statements
  • Drafting credit memos and committee summaries
02 / LINE MANAGEMENT

Line assignment and limit strategy

Initial line, line increase and line decrease strategies, risk/reward optimization and controlled experiments.

Where AI helps
  • Rationale text for adverse action and review files
  • Monitoring narratives
03 / REPORTING & ANALYTICS

Board and committee reporting

Board, ALCO and credit committee packs, CECL allowance support, portfolio and concentration reporting, and stress testing.

Where AI helps
  • First-draft commentary from query results
  • Natural-language access to approved data
04 / AML/BSA & FRAUD

Sharper monitoring, faster casework

Transaction-monitoring tuning, alert triage, KYC/CDD refresh and model-based detection.

Where AI helps
  • Alert summaries and SAR narrative drafts for analyst review
  • Adverse-media screening
05 / OPERATIONS

Back-office workflow automation

Mapping and redesign of loan servicing, account opening, exception handling and back-office workflows.

Where AI helps
  • Document intake and classification
  • Procedure Q&A assistants, ticket and email routing
06 / MODEL RISK & AI GOVERNANCE

Governance for every model you run

Model inventory, validation, ongoing performance monitoring, AI use policy and vendor model reviews.

Where AI helps
  • Documentation drafting and evidence collection

Why Risk Prism AI

Fold the path.
Keep the picture upright.

The prism inside a pair of binoculars adds nothing to the light. It folds a long optical path into a compact instrument and turns an inverted image right side up, so what you see is sharp, magnified and the right way round.

That is the job we take on. AI shortens the path: language models and statistical methods applied only where they measurably save time or improve decisions. Risk discipline keeps the picture upright: every tool is treated as a model under your risk framework, tested against ground truth, documented for examiners and owned by your staff.

01

Assess 2–4 weeks

Map processes, data, systems and controls. Find where automation pays off and where it adds risk.

Deliverable: a prioritized opportunity map with estimated time savings and risk impact.

02

Pilot 6–10 weeks

Build one solution on a contained use case with real data, run it in parallel with the current process, and measure accuracy, cycle time and exception rates.

Deliverable: a working pilot, validation results and governance documentation.

03

Deploy

Integrate with your core, loan origination, AML and reporting platforms. Train users; write procedures and controls.

Deliverable: a production solution and operating runbook.

04

Monitor and transfer

Ongoing performance monitoring, periodic recalibration and annual review support, with knowledge transfer so your staff own the solution.

You make a go/no-go decision at the end of every phase.

AI you can defend

Built to be explained
to examiners and boards.

Every LLM-based or statistical tool we deliver is treated as a model or critical process under your risk management framework, scaled to your size and to the tool's materiality. Documentation, audit trails and monitoring are built in from the start, not retrofitted before an exam.

MEASURED

Tested against
ground truth

LLM output is scored with quantitative metrics (accuracy, error rates, calibrated uncertainty intervals) before and after deployment. It is not judged by impression, and not by another LLM alone.

ACCOUNTABLE

People decide.
AI assists.

No credit denial rests on an LLM alone, and analysts keep the call on alert dispositions and SAR filings. Credit solutions are tested for disparate impact and support specific, accurate adverse action reasons under ECOA/Regulation B and FCRA.

CONTROLLED

Documented, monitored,
protected

Plain-language documentation of what each tool does, its limitations and its controls. Customer data is handled under GLBA and your information security program, and it is never used to train third-party models without your written consent.

Vendor-neutral, data stays home

We work with the platforms you already run and favor deployments that keep customer data in your environment or an approved private cloud, using commercial or open-weight models as data-protection and cost requirements dictate. We accept no undisclosed referral fees or commissions from vendors.

Scoped to your budget, built to hand over

Fixed-fee assessments and pilots, project fees for deployment, and optional retainers for monitoring, validation or fractional chief risk and analytics officer support. The principals assigned to your engagement do the work, then train your staff to run it without us.
